## Features

- **AI-Powered Editing Tools** – Presets and templates for Generate Captions (automatic audio transcription and subtitle placement in the timeline)[reference:0], Edit Detection (automatically splits rendered video back into original clips)[reference:1], Auto Mask (AI recognition of skin, hair, sky, foliage, and clothing without manual tracking)[reference:2], and Smart Conform for automatic square/vertical delivery[reference:3]
- **Magnetic Timeline & Organization** – Templates leveraging advanced metadata and Clip Connections for faster editing[reference:4], plus Enhanced Timeline Index for drag-and-drop audio role rearrangement[reference:5]
- **Color Grading Workflows** – Presets for Magnetic Mask (AI-powered isolation of people, objects, and shapes from footage)[reference:6], enhanced Match Color for accurate colour matches across varied footage[reference:7], and HDR color spaces including Rec. 2020 and HLG[reference:8]
- **Adjustment Clips** – Templates for applying color corrections and effects across multiple clips simultaneously using adjustment clips above the timeline[reference:9][reference:10]
- **Professional Audio Editing** – Presets for integrated Logic-based effects, Voice Isolation for speech enhancement[reference:11], Quantec QRS room simulator for natural-sounding reverbs[reference:12], and multi-channel audio editing[reference:13]
- **Media Organization** – Templates for library-based media management, proxy creation, Smart Collections, and duplicate media detection[reference:14]
- **Export & Publishing Automation** – Presets for fast export to Apple devices, Vimeo, YouTube, audio stems, and multiple versions using roles metadata[reference:15]
- **Cross-Application Integration** – Workflows for sending key frames directly to Pixelmator Pro and opening images from Keynote, Pages, and Numbers[reference:16]
- **Performance Optimization** – Configurations optimised for Apple Silicon, ProRes, ProRes RAW, RED, XAVC, and more[reference:17]
- **Step-by-step documentation** with visual guides and troubleshooting tips

## Usage

1. Download and extract the repository files.
2. Open the `/docs` folder and read the `GettingStarted.md` guide.
3. Choose your desired configuration:
   - **Templates:** Import the workflow and project templates from `/templates` via Final Cut Pro's File > Import.
   - **Scripts:** Run the automation scripts from `/scripts` using Final Cut Pro's built-in scripting or third-party tools.
   - **Presets:** Apply the color grading, audio, and export presets from `/presets`.
4. Launch Final Cut Pro and follow the documentation to apply your configurations.
